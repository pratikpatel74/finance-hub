Borrowing Products

APR, overdrafts, credit cards, personal loans, payday loans and credit history.

1. APR — The Cost of Borrowing

  • APR (Annual Percentage Rate) — standardised measure including interest rate + compulsory fees, as a yearly %. Required in all adverts.
  • Representative APR — the rate offered to at least 51% of successful applicants. Your actual rate may be higher.
  • EAR (Equivalent Annual Rate) — used specifically for overdrafts. Since 2020, must also be quoted as APR.

2. Overdrafts

✅
Arranged (Authorised)
Pre-agreed limit; charged at a set EAR/APR. Cheaper option.
⚠️
Unarranged (Unauthorised)
Over balance without agreement. Since 2020 FCA rules: single interest rate, no daily fees. More expensive.
Interest is calculated daily: (overdraft amount × annual rate) ÷ 365. When overdrawn, the bank is creditor, you are debtor.

Card brands (Visa, Mastercard) provide the network between acquirer and issuer.

  • Interest-free period: up to 56 days if full balance paid monthly. Interest applies from transaction date if only partial payment made.
  • Minimum repayment: typically 1–2% of balance or £25 (whichever greater). Paying only the minimum incurs significant interest.
  • Section 75 (Consumer Credit Act 1974): card issuer jointly liable for purchases of £100–£30,000 if goods/services not delivered or faulty.

5. Personal Loans

  • Fixed sum, repaid in equal monthly instalments over agreed term (typically 1–7 years).
  • Typical amounts: £1,000–£25,000. Fixed APR — no surprises on repayments.
  • Unsecured: no collateral required. Early repayment permitted (penalty up to 2 months' interest).
  • Best for: large one-off purchases (car, home improvement) where structured repayment is preferred.

Payday loans can carry 1,000%+ representative APR due to the very short term. The FCA caps prevent the debt escalating out of control, but they remain high-risk.

7. Credit Reference Agencies (CRAs)

  • Three main UK CRAs: Experian, Equifax, TransUnion (formerly Callcredit).
  • Credit file holds: payment history, outstanding debts, electoral roll, CCJs, credit applications. Held for 6 years.
  • CCJ (County Court Judgment): if ordered repayment unpaid within one month → registered for 6 years, significantly harming credit score.
  • Poor credit history = higher APRs or outright rejection. Good history = better rates.
