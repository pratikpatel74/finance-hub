Consumer Protection

PRA, FCA, FOS, FSCS, CMA and the post–credit crunch regulatory landscape.

1. The Credit Crunch (2007–09)

  • Sub-prime lending — US banks lent mortgages to high-risk customers (NINJA loans: No Income, No Job or Assets) at low introductory rates.
  • Cross-contamination — retail banking profits used to fund losses from risky investment banking; depositors' money put at risk.
  • "Too big to fail" — largest banks so systemically important that failure would damage the entire economy.

2. The Regulatory Landscape

PRA
Prudential Regulation Authority

Part of Bank of England since April 2013. Ensures financial safety and soundness of banks, building societies, credit unions, insurers. Micro-prudential: forward-looking, judgement-based.

FCA
Financial Conduct Authority

Independent; replaced FSA from April 2013. Three objectives: consumer protection, market integrity, effective competition. Enforces fines, withdraws authorisations, orders compensation.

FOS
Financial Ombudsman Service

Independent dispute resolution. Free to consumers. Provider must respond within 8 weeks, then customer can refer to FOS within 6 months. FOS decisions binding on providers, not customers.

FSCS
Financial Services Compensation Scheme

Compensates if an FCA-authorised provider fails. £85,000 per person per authorised firm. Joint accounts: each holder covered to £85k. Refund within 7 days of failure. 98% of UK population under the limit.

CMA
Competition and Markets Authority

Independent; since April 2014. Investigates anti-competitive mergers, cartels, market studies. Led Open Banking reforms 2017 — banks must share data via APIs (with consent).

5. PPI Scandal

  • PPI (Payment Protection Insurance) — insurance sold alongside loans/credit cards to cover repayments if the holder became unable to work.
  • Widely mis-sold: sold to people who didn't need it or couldn't claim; pressured add-on.
  • By 2021: over £38 billion in compensation paid out — largest financial mis-selling scandal in UK history.
  • FCA complaint deadline: 29 August 2019. No new PPI complaints accepted after this date.
