  • Budget: plan of expected income and expenditure (usually monthly). Helps live within your means.
  • Cash flow forecast: estimate of future inflows and outflows; identifies months where expenditure may exceed income.
  • Living within your means: income ≥ expenditure. Consistently spending more leads to debt accumulation.

3. Inflation & Real Value

  • CPI (Consumer Prices Index) — official UK measure of inflation; tracks a 'basket' of goods and services. Published monthly by the ONS.
  • Real vs nominal: AER on savings must exceed CPI rate for savings to grow in real terms.
  • When inflation > wage growth → real purchasing power falls (you can buy less with the same money).

6. Problem Debt & Support

  • CCJ (County Court Judgment) — issued when creditor sues for unpaid debt. Stays on credit file for 6 years. Can lead to bailiff action.
  • DMP (Debt Management Plan) — informal arrangement managed by a debt charity; single monthly payment distributed among creditors.
  • IVA (Individual Voluntary Arrangement) — legally binding agreement to pay back a portion of debts over 5–6 years. Alternative to bankruptcy.
  • Bankruptcy — legal process; debts may be written off after 1 year but severe consequences for credit and assets.
  • StepChange / MoneyHelper — free debt advice. MoneyHelper run by MaPS (Money and Pensions Service), formerly Money Advice Service.

7. Insurance

Mandatory
Car insurance — legally required (Road Traffic Act 1988). Minimum: third-party cover.
Voluntary
Buildings & contents, life insurance, income protection, travel insurance — advisable but not legally required.
  • Income protection — replaces a proportion of income if you can't work due to illness or injury.
  • Excess — the amount you pay towards each claim. Higher excess = lower premium.
  • PPI (Payment Protection Insurance) — widely mis-sold alongside loans/credit cards; subject to UK's largest mis-selling scandal (£38bn+ compensation).
