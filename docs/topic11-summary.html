2. Income Tax Bands (2024/25)

Income Tax Visual

40%

Worked example — Salary £30,000: Taxable income = £30,000 − £12,570 = £17,430. Tax = £17,430 × 20% = £3,486. You do NOT pay 20% on the full £30,000.
  • PAYE — Pay As You Earn: employer deducts income tax and NI before paying you.
  • Tax code — e.g. 1257L = standard personal allowance. Tells employer how much to allow before tax.
  • Self Assessment — for self-employment, rental income, complex affairs. Online deadline: 31 January following tax year end.
  • Tax year: 6 April – 5 April (e.g. 2024/25: 6 April 2024 – 5 April 2025).
